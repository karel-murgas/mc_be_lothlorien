## Spike: two variants

Vanilla moved "has nectar" from the `minecraft:is_charged` component to the property `minecraft:has_nectar` in 1.19.50,
so the hive probably reads that property for honey. The variants differ only in the nectar property:

- **A** `lothlorien:mallorn_bee` declares vanilla's `minecraft:has_nectar`. Risk: a custom entity may not be allowed a
  `minecraft:` property (then A does not load; the content log says why).
- **B** `lothlorien:mallorn_bee_b` ("Mallorn Bee (test B)") declares `lothlorien:has_nectar`.

After the test, keep the winner in `VARIANTS` in the generator, delete the other's files and lang lines.

## Spike test (in game, existing world is fine)

Setup: creative, flat spot in daylight, a `minecraft:beehive` (empty) on a post, a patch of Elanor 3-5 blocks away.
Content log on (Settings > Creator). Spawn 2 of each variant with the eggs, nearby but apart.

1. Both eggs exist and spawn a pale gold bee with silver stripes (A missing = property refused; note the log line).
2. Bees fly to Elanor, hover on it, then show nectar (pollen-dusted texture, dripping particles).
3. With nectar they go into the hive. They come out as the same variant (gold, right name on a name tag).
4. Honey: after several nectar trips the hive fills (`/time add` helps; level 5 shows honey on the front). Note which
   variant raises the honey level. **This picks the winner.**
5. Night (`/time set night`): bees go into the hive; at day they come out again.
6. Break the hive (no silk touch): bees get angry, sting, poison, die after stinging. Shears and a glass bottle on a
   full hive give honeycomb / a honey bottle.
7. A vanilla bee in the same hive: both come and go, no errors.
8. Breed two Mallorn bees with Elanor; the baby is a small gold bee. Tempt with Niphredil.
9. Do our bees buzz (the loop sound)?

Results go here with the date and game version, then the plan's "plan A / plan B" decision.

### Round 1 (2026-10-02, 1.26.52, owner)

- Both variants load and spawn (so a custom entity may declare `minecraft:has_nectar`).
- At night both went into a vanilla beehive **and** a bee nest, and came out at sunrise (step 5 passes). By day they ignored
  the hives, which is vanilla behaviour without nectar.
- **They ignored Elanor and Niphredil** (bare ids in `target_blocks`), so no nectar and no honey test yet.
  Suspected cause: a bare id matches only the default state; flowers on grass have `lothlorien:on_grass=true`.
  Round 2 lists every exact state as a block descriptor `{"name", "states"}` (38 entries).

### Round 2 (2026-10-02, 1.26.52, owner)

Flowers on dirt (default state) were ignored with bare ids too, and the exact-state descriptors (new world, new nests,
new bees) changed nothing. The block-state theory is wrong; descriptors reverted.

### Round 3 test (one cause per variant, `SPIKE_FLOWER_GOAL` in the generator)

Spawn the bees right next to the flowers: the flower search reaches only 6 blocks sideways and 4 up/down.
- **Mallorn Bee**: our flowers only, vanilla `is_waterlogged` target filter removed.
- **Mallorn Bee (test B)**: filter kept, our flowers **plus poppy and dandelion** as a control.

| Seen | Means |

| Test B ignores the poppy too | the flower goal never runs for our bee (group/event issue) |
